DraftHR · Onboarding funnel design

Compare the options.

Five mockups to evaluate two design decisions. Open each in a new tab, see how it feels, then decide together. No code committed against any of these yet — pure evaluation.

Decision 1 · Marketing page approach

Option A

Single signup card

One card on app.drafthr.io with "I'm in HR" / "I need help with my employer" buttons. Smallest surface area, ships immediately, no separate marketing site.

In-app~1 hour
Option B

/welcome/hr + /welcome/employee

Richer landing pages per intent, served from the same app at app.drafthr.io/welcome/*. Same Vercel project. Adds value props and social proof before signup.

In-app~half-day
Option C

Full marketing site at drafthr.io

Proper marketing site at the apex domain. Lives in /web/ (already exists), separate concerns from the app. Best for SEO + paid acquisition. Mock shows a Linear-quality landing.

Recommended for growth~2 days

Decision 2 · Employee checkout softeners

Softener 1

30-second product preview

Before Stripe checkout, show an animated preview of the wizard + a sample exported letter. They see WHAT they're paying for before paying. Higher conversion, no free product given away.

Pre-payment
Softener 2

24-hour money-back guarantee

Stripe checkout with explicit "If your letter doesn't help, full refund within 24h" badge. Removes "what if I waste £99" anxiety. Low refund rate in practice for high-stakes work.

Post-payment

What you're deciding

Decision 1 sets where the brand-building work lives (in-app vs separate marketing site) and how much SEO surface area we have. Option C is the long-term winner but needs more upfront work.

Decision 2 affects the Employee funnel only. We've already locked "pay from the jump." These soften the upfront-pay shock without giving free product. Pick both, one, or neither.
