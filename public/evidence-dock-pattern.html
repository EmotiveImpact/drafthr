Evidence — dock pattern

State 1 · Rest — twin pills at bottom-right

Default state. Evidence and Ask AI are both docks — twin pills at bottom-right, low-weight, symmetric. No permanent right rail eating letter width. Either can be clicked open into a 420×600 drawer, either can be pinned to a full-height rail. Only one rail at a time — pinning Evidence collapses Ask AI back to a pill, and vice versa. Forces the user to declare the dominant work mode.

Why kill the permanent AI rail? It costs ~320px of letter width 100% of the time, but users only consult AI suggestions in bursts. Pill-on-demand respects the writing surface; pinning is the escape hatch for AI-heavy sessions.

💡 One dock pattern, two payloads. Same behaviours, same chrome, same gestures. Evidence and Ask AI are interchangeable from a UX standpoint — only the contents differ.

Wins of the unified dock model

• Letter gets its width back. No permanent ~320px right rail at rest — full canvas for writing.
• Symmetry is teachable. Learn the pattern once, applies to both docks. Future docks inherit it.
• Pin = mode declaration. User explicitly says "this session is about evidence" or "about AI" by pinning one.
• Mobile-graceful. Same pattern collapses to a single bottom-sheet on small screens.
• Familiar. LinkedIn Messaging, Gmail chat, Intercom — bottom-right docks are a known pattern.
• Future-proof. Comments, Notifications, Messaging can join the dock tray without UI churn.

Costs

• Loss of always-on AI suggestions. Users who relied on the right-rail glance must now click the pill. Mitigation: pill shows a subtle badge when AI has a new suggestion.
• Mutually exclusive pinning may frustrate power users who want both rails up. But two simultaneous rails would squeeze the letter to ~600px — too narrow for serif body copy.
• New gestures to teach — expand-fullheight, collapse-to-pill, pin/unpin (tooltip on first hover).
• Bottom-of-screen real estate reserved for docks — future toasts/snackbars must clear this zone.

Migration path — 5 commits

  1. Extract a reusable <Dock> shell. One component that handles pill ↔ drawer ↔ rail state transitions. Slots for header, body, footer. Used by both Evidence and Ask AI.
  2. Convert EvidenceDrawer to use Dock. Existing content moves into the Dock body. Drawer dimensions become 420×600 bottom-right (was Radix Sheet right-side overlay).
  3. Convert AI rail to use Dock. Remove the permanent right column. AI suggestions render inside the Dock body. Pill appears at bottom-right alongside Evidence.
  4. Add mutually-exclusive pin state. Pinning one dock unpins the other (with a 200ms transition). State stored per-draft in localStorage as { pinnedDock: 'evidence' | 'ai' | null }.
  5. Reorder toolbar. Invite pill before Review CTA. Top-bar Evidence and Ask AI buttons removed (the pills replace them).
