What changed:
  • Left rail = Required-evidence checklist (was Sections list)
  • Each row carries required / optional / done state, the expectation label, and which section it belongs to
  • Clicking a row scrolls the canvas to that requirement's upload area (and selects the relevant section behind the scenes)
  • "X / Y" header count = how many requirements satisfied
  • The previous "Required evidence" card at the top of the canvas is gone — it lived in two places before; now there's one source of truth
  • The canvas focuses on the active requirement + other evidence already collected for that section
Net win: the rail is action-oriented ("what do I still need?") instead of structural ("which section?"). The structural info is implied — each item knows its section.
