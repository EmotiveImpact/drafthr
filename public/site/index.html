How it works

From a blank page to a letter you can stand behind.

Step 1 of 7
  1. Step 1 · Telling

    Tell us what’s happening

    Write it in your own words, the way you would tell a colleague. DraftHR reads it and works out what kind of situation it is, where you are and which side you are on.

    • No forms to fill in before you start
    • For employers, employees and HR consultants
    • UK and US rules
  2. Step 2 · Asking

    Answer the questions that matter

    Then it asks only what this case needs, one question at a time, and builds a case file from your answers as you go.

    • Only the questions this case needs
    • Every answer saved to the case file
    • Gaps and clashes flagged before you draft
  3. Step 3 · Planning

    Get the fair process

    You see every step the process needs for your situation, what is already done, and the letter you need next.

    • Each step explained in plain English
    • What is done and what comes next
    • Matched to the rules where the case is
  4. Step 4 · Drafting

    Draft

    DraftHR writes each letter section by section from the case file, so the names, facts and dates are already right.

    • Filled from the case file, never retyped
    • Change any section on its own
    • 35 letter types for the UK and US
  5. Step 5 · Reviewing

    Review

    Before it goes out, a teammate, someone you trust or an employment law expert checks it. You accept or reject every change.

    • Invite a teammate or someone you trust
    • Or book an employment law expert
    • Nothing changes without your say
  6. Step 6 · Time limits

    Deadlines

    DraftHR works out the dates that matter, from appeal windows to tribunal time limits, and reminds you before they pass.

    • Tribunal time limits worked out for you
    • Appeal and reply dates tracked
    • Reminders before a date passes
  7. Step 7 · Records

    Records

    Every answer, upload and letter is saved with its date and who added it, so you have a clear record if a decision is questioned.

    • Saved with the date and who added it
    • Never overwritten
    • Ready if a decision is questioned

Questions

The things people usually ask before their first letter.

Is DraftHR legal advice?

No. DraftHR drafts, you decide. For complex or high stakes matters, speak to a qualified employment adviser, or book an HR expert through the DraftHR marketplace.

Does it cover the UK and the US?

Yes. Choose where you work when you start, and the letter follows the right structure and terms for it.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Always. Edit any section yourself, or describe the change you want and apply it only if you are happy with it.

Can someone check my letter before I send it?

Yes. Share it with a teammate or someone you trust, or choose an HR expert from the marketplace. You can go back and forth until it is right.

I am an employee. Do I need a plan?

No. Employees can draft a single letter from £9.99 without a subscription.
