Answering questions

Everything explained to help you start

Is DraftHR legal advice?

No. DraftHR drafts, you decide. For complex or high stakes matters, speak to a qualified employment adviser.

Does it cover the UK and the US?

Yes. Choose the jurisdiction when you start and the letter follows the right structure and terms for it.

Which letters can I write?

Grievances, resignations, settlement responses, flexible working requests and more, 35 letter templates in all.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Always. Edit any section directly, or describe the change you want and apply it only if you are happy with it.

I am an employee. Do I need a plan?

No. Employees can draft a single letter from £9.99 without a subscription.

What do AI critique credits do?

A critique reviews a whole draft and flags where it could be stronger before you send it. Pro includes 20 a month.
