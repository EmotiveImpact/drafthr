How it works

From blank page to signed off, in four steps.

01Draft

Pick one of 35 templates and answer a few plain questions. DraftHR writes a first version in the right structure.

02Review

Work through it section by section. Ask for changes in your own words and use AI critique to find weak points.

03Approve

Every AI change is a proposal. Nothing reaches the letter until you apply it, and teams can route it through an approval queue.

04Send

Export a clean letter ready to email, print or attach, with an evidence pack when you need one.

What is DraftHR?

An AI drafting tool for HR letters in the UK and the US. It helps HR teams, employees and consultants write letters that are clear, well structured and ready to send.

Is DraftHR legal advice?

No. DraftHR drafts, you decide. For complex or high stakes matters, speak to a qualified employment adviser.

Which letters can I write?

Grievances, resignations, settlement responses, flexible working requests and more, 35 letter templates in all.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Always. Edit any section directly, or describe the change you want and apply it only if you are happy with it.

I am an employee. Do I need a plan?

No. Employees can draft a single letter from £9.99 without a subscription.
