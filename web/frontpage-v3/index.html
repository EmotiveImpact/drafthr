What DraftHR is

DraftHR drafts the letters that matter at work: grievances, settlement replies, flexible working requests and resignations. Each one is built in the right structure for the UK or the US, and nothing leaves until you approve it.

For HR teams who need every manager to write the same way, for employees putting their case in writing, and for consultants drafting on behalf of clients. 35 letter templates, from £9.99 a letter.

Anatomy of a letter

A formal grievance, in the order it will be read

Every template breaks the letter into sections, so the facts, the basis and the outcome you want are each set out on their own.

  1. Date and recipient
  2. Subject
  3. Salutation
  4. Statement
  5. Background
  6. Witnesses
  7. Protected basis
  8. Impact
  9. Outcome
  10. Hearing request
  11. Reservation
  12. Closing
  13. Signature
