What is DraftHR?

An AI drafting tool for HR letters in the UK and the US. It helps HR teams, employees and consultants write letters that are clear, well structured and ready to send.

Is DraftHR legal advice?

No. DraftHR drafts, you decide. For complex or high stakes matters, speak to a qualified employment adviser, or book one through the DraftHR marketplace.

Does it cover the UK and the US?

Yes. Choose the jurisdiction when you start and the letter follows the right structure and terms for it.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Always. Edit any section directly, or describe the change you want and apply it only if you are happy with it.

Can someone check my letter before I send it?

Yes. Send it to a teammate or someone you trust, or choose an HR expert from the marketplace. Go back and forth until it is right.

I am an employee. Do I need a plan?

No. Employees can draft a single letter from £9.99 without a subscription.
